The Challenge

Second-Hand Retail is Growing.
The Operations Haven't Caught Up.

Independent thrift and consignment stores are drowning in manual inventory work while the resale market booms globally.

❌ The Old Way

Manual Work is Killing Margins

  • Staff spend 15–20 minutes per item on photos, categorization & data entry
  • Only 45% of physical inventory ever gets digitized and published online
  • Stock errors create bad customer experiences and lost sales opportunities
  • Small retailers cannot afford enterprise software or custom workflows
✅ The i-Stock Way

AI Does the Heavy Lifting

  • Upload a single photo — AI removes background & enhances the image automatically
  • System assigns category, title, description, tags & price — zero typing
  • Human-in-the-loop: staff review and approve, AI handles everything else
  • Real-time sync to i-Buy storefront — customers see items in seconds
